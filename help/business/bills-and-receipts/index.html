Bills and receipts

Aubloo works out each booking's bill from your rates. At check-out you check it, record what you were handed, and the customer is emailed a receipt.

How a bill is worked out

Each booking has one bill. It comes from your rates (nights, days or hours, and meals), any grooms and other charges, less any discount. A groom done during a stay goes on the stay's bill. Prices are in rupees and include tax; Aubloo does not add GST. If you have saved your GST number, it is printed on the receipt.

Before check-out, the customer sees the bill as an estimate: the final amount is settled at check-out.

At check-out

When you check a pet out (see Check-in and check-out), the sheet … is going home opens.

  1. Check the bill. We worked out shows Aubloo's figure. Adjust Meals eaten with One more or One fewer if needed.
  2. Correct anything. Under Charges, you can change the rates, add Other charges, give a Discount (off the whole booking) or type the Total you agreed. Any change needs a Comment, which the customer reads with the bill. Then tap Update the bill.
  3. Say what was handed over. Amount received is filled in with what is owed; change it to what you were actually given. Add Remarks if you like.
  4. Tap Close booking.

Aubloo records the amount only. Payment happens outside the app, by whatever means you accept, and the app does not ask how it was paid.

  • If less was received, the rest is marked as due against the customer.
  • A Business Admin can instead tick Write off what is left, so nothing is carried forward.
  • You cannot record more than the booking came to.

If you tap Not now, the pet is still checked out and the amount stays owed by the customer; the app tells you to record it later under Customers & pets → the customer → Record a payment. If the person checking the pet out cannot take payment, the bill stays open on Money owed for someone who can.

Dues paid later

Your home screen shows what is still owed to you. Open it (or the customer's Still to settle), tap Record a payment, type what you were handed and choose which bookings it goes on. The customer is told in the app and by email that you received it, and how much is still owing.

Receipts

When a booking is closed, the customer is emailed a receipt with a PDF attached, as long as you have their email address. It lists the items, the bill total, what was received, anything waived and anything still owing. Receipts are not sent by SMS.

On a closed booking you can tap Open receipt to see the PDF, or Send the receipt again. If the customer has no email on file, add one first.

Changing a closed bill

A bill can be corrected until the booking is closed. After that it is what the customer was charged: it cannot be edited, reopened or voided. Check the figures before you tap Close booking.
