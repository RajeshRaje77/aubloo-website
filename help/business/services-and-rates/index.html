Services and rates

Choose what you sell, set what each thing costs and say how many pets you can take. Everything here is in Settings, the gear icon at the top of your screens. Settings is a list of groups (for example Boarding & daycare and Your business); tap a group to open its rows. Only a Business Admin can change these.

The services Aubloo knows

  • Boarding & daycare: overnight stays, and day guests who go home the same day. This is one switch; the customer picks boarding or daycare when they book.
  • Grooming: your own menu, prices and grooming tables. Dog businesses only.
  • Foster care: longer stays with their own rate and their own places. Dog businesses only.

Cat businesses can offer boarding and daycare. Grooming and foster care are not offered for cats.

A service shows in the app only when it is switched on and your Aubloo plan covers it.

Switch a service on or off

  1. Open Settings → Your business → Services you offer.
  2. Turn the switch for each service on or off, then tap Save.
  3. When you turn one on, a Next card takes you to its setup, for example Set up boarding & daycare or Set up grooming.

Switching a service off stops new bookings for it. Anything already booked still runs, and is checked out and billed as normal. Your prices and menu are kept as you left them.

A business has to offer at least one service, so you cannot switch off the last one.

Your subscription still charges for a service you switch off. To change your plan, ask Aubloo under Settings → Subscription. If your plan does not cover a service, Aubloo says so when you try to switch it on.

Boarding and daycare rates

Open Settings → Boarding & daycare → Pricing & capacity.

Choose how you charge

  • Boarding: by the nights & days (Monday to Thursday is 3 nights and 4 days; set a night rate, a day rate or both, and whichever you fill is charged) or by the hour (part hours count as full hours).
  • Daycare: by the full day (one rate for the day; a half day is adjusted on the bill) or by the hour.

Switching how you charge clears the rates you set for the other way, and Aubloo asks you for the new ones straight away.

Set the rates

  1. Tap Set boarding rate (or the boarding card) and fill For 1 night, For 1 day or For 1 hour.
  2. If you charge for food separately, fill For 1 meal. It is charged on how many meals are actually eaten. Leave it blank if meals are not charged separately.
  3. Do the same for daycare: For 1 full day or For 1 hour, and the meal rate.
  4. Tap Save.

A blank rate means "not set"; 0 is a real price. If every boarding rate is blank, boarding is not shown to your customers until it has one.

Capacity

In the same sheet, Capacity (boarding & daycare) is how many pets you can physically house at once.

Bookings stop at the smaller of your capacity and what your plan covers. The Your plan line under the card tells you which applies. For example, "Your plan covers 10 at once, so bookings stop there — upgrade to use all 12".

You cannot lower capacity below the number of pets already booked on a day. Aubloo tells you which day.

Foster care

Open Settings → Foster care → Foster rates & places. Set For 1 night and/or For 1 day (a foster stay is at least one night), an optional For 1 meal, and Foster places.

Foster places are a separate pool. Foster stays do not use your boarding space, and boarding does not use theirs.

Grooming menu

  1. Open Settings → Grooming → Grooming menu & tables and tap Add a service.
  2. Give it a Name (for example "Full groom"). Optionally, say What's included; customers see this.
  3. Under How it is priced, choose One price or By size. With sizes, leave a size blank if you do not offer it.
  4. Pick How long it takes, in 30-minute steps.
  5. If it can also be added on top of another groom, turn on Can be added to another groom.
  6. Under Done on, tick which tables can do it. A service no table does cannot be booked.
  7. Tap Add to menu.

Sizes are your own names (for example Small, Medium, Large). Weights in kg are optional: they suggest a size from a pet's weight, and whoever books can change it.

Tables are where grooms happen. Name each one, choose Everything on the menu or Only some services, and set any days off. Set the days and times grooms can be booked under Grooming hours.

Changes apply to new bookings. Anything already booked keeps its price and length. Retiring a service takes it off the menu for new bookings.

Tax and your GST number

Prices are what the customer pays, in rupees, including any tax. Aubloo does not add GST to a bill. To print your GST number on your bills, open Settings → Your business → GST number and enter the 15 characters from your GST registration certificate. Aubloo checks the format, so a typo is caught. Leave it blank to take it off your bills.

Days you are closed

Open Settings → Your business → Blocked dates → Block days.

  1. Pick the days and tap Next.
  2. Tick what is unavailable: Stays, Foster care or Grooming.
  3. Add a reason if you like (only you see it), then tap Block it.

Nothing can be booked on a blocked date. Bookings already taken stay booked and nothing is cancelled. Their customers get a message saying you will call, and Aubloo gives you the list of who to call. To tell every customer about a closure, use Announce: they get a notice in the app and by email. To open the days again, tap Reopen.
