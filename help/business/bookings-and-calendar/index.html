Bookings and the calendar

Customers ask for bookings in their app and you answer them. Your team can also book directly. The calendar is where you run each day.

Make a booking

  1. Open Bookings and tap New booking.
  2. Who is it for? Search by name, pet's name or mobile, or add a new customer.
  3. Which pets? Tick one or more. If a pet's profile is incomplete or a vaccine is missing, you are told. You can still book, and add the proof now or ask at drop-off.
  4. What for? Pick the service. Only services with a price can be booked.
  5. When? Choose They're here now or Pick a day, set drop-off and pick-up, and, if you charge for meals, whether the pet eats your food or their own. The pick-up day does not count as a night.
  6. Check the price (an estimate, settled at check-out) and tap Confirm, or Confirm & check in if the pet is already here.

A booking made by your team is confirmed straight away. Booking needs the Create bookings right (see Staff).

Capacity is a hard limit. Aubloo never books past your capacity, even for staff. If a day is full, the app shows Over capacity on … and offers another day or fewer nights; a Business Admin can also upgrade the plan.

Nothing can be booked on a blocked date. A day you are normally closed only shows a warning.

Answer booking requests

Open Approvals from your home screen. It shows how many requests are waiting. Under Booking requests, each card shows the dates, pets and services, with flags for anything to check, such as capacity, vaccinations or a do-not-rebook flag. Nothing is held for the customer until you answer.

  • Approve: add a note if you like, then Confirm booking. The customer is told.
  • Can't take it: say why (the customer sees it), then Refuse booking.
  • For a groom, Approve at another time lets you offer a different slot. Call the customer, then mark it confirmed.

A request whose day has passed moves to Lapsed. You can bring it back with Revive with a new date.

Find and change a booking

Bookings lists every booking under Upcoming, In progress and Finished, with a search by pet or customer name. On a booking's card in the calendar, More actions lets you change the dates or time, notify the owner for pick-up, see the bill, record an incident, mark a no-show, or cancel.

  • Cancel booking needs a reason, which the customer sees. You cannot cancel once the pet is checked in or in grooming.
  • No-show is available from the booked day, for a pet that did not arrive.
  • Customers can cancel in their app up to the day before the booking starts.

The calendar

From Bookings, tap Calendar, or tap a day in the week strip.

  • The week strip shows each day and how many of your places are booked. Move a week with the arrows at either end, and tap See the day for the day's numbers.
  • Each pet's card shows its customer, service and dates, with the next step (Check in or Discharge) and More actions. A stay past its last day is marked Overdue.
  • The magnifier finds a booking by pet or customer name; the calendar icon next to it opens the list of all bookings.
  • A business with more than one service can show one service at a time. For stays you can then switch between Day and Next 14 days and use Book here; grooming shows each table's day, where you start, finish, move, confirm by phone, or close and bill a groom.

Each card's button moves the pet along: Check in, then Discharge. See Check-in and check-out.
