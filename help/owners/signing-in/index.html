Signing in

As a pet owner, your mobile number is your Aubloo account. There is no password: each time you sign in, Aubloo sends you a 6-digit code by SMS and WhatsApp, or by email.

Sign in with your mobile

  1. Open Aubloo and choose Pet owner.
  2. In Your mobile, type your 10-digit mobile number. Use the number your pet business has for you. You do not need to type +91; Aubloo adds it.
  3. Tap Send OTP. You will see We sent a 6-digit code to your mobile. (The screen also says You'll get booking updates on WhatsApp. You can turn this off in My profile.)
  4. Type the code from the SMS or WhatsApp message into 6-digit OTP and tap Sign in.

Good to know: a code is valid for 10 minutes. Only Indian (+91) mobile numbers can sign in today.

The first time you sign in with a new number, Aubloo creates your account. You do not need a business to add you first.

Sign in with an email code

If you have added an email address to your account (see below), you can get your code by email instead, for example when SMS and WhatsApp messages are not reaching you.

  1. On the sign-in screen, tap Use an email code.
  2. Type your email and tap Send OTP. You will see We emailed you a 6-digit OTP.
  3. Type the code into 6-digit OTP and tap Sign in.

To go back to your mobile, tap Use mobile.

An email code only works for an email you have already confirmed in the app. It never creates a new account. To add one: open My profile, tap Sign-in email, and confirm the code Aubloo sends to that address. With an email you can always get back in — even when texts do not arrive.

After you sign in

  • If a pet business has already added you, Aubloo may show We found a record that may be yours. Tap That's me if it is, or Not mine.
  • If you are not with a business yet, you will see Find your pet business. See Joining a business.

"Welcome back": naming one of your pets

If you have not signed in for 90 days or more and your account has no email address, Aubloo asks you to type the name of one of your pets before it lets you in. It is there to keep your pets' records safe.

After three wrong answers, the app asks you to call the business that looks after your pet: they can confirm your number and let you back in.

If your mobile number changes

You cannot change your mobile number yourself. It is how your pet business knows you, so the business changes it:

  1. Ask your pet business to change your number. They send a code to your new number.
  2. On the sign-in screen, tap My pet business changed my number.
  3. Enter Your new number and the 6-digit code, then tap Confirm.
  4. Your account now signs in with the new number. Tap Sign in and sign in with it.

That code also lasts 10 minutes. If it has run out, ask your pet business to send a new one.

Signing out

Pet owners stay signed in until they sign out; the app does not sign you out for being idle.

  • This phone: tap the sign-out icon at the top of the screen, then Sign out.
  • Every phone: open My profile and tap Sign out all devices, then Sign out all. Every phone signed in to your account is signed out, this one too.

Code not arriving? See I did not get a sign-in code.
