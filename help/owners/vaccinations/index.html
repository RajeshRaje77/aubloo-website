Vaccination records

You add a photo or PDF of each vaccination certificate. The business reads the date off it and records it, so your pet's record stays right.

Add a certificate

  1. Open your pet's vaccinations: on the pet's card tap About …, then Edit, the Vaccinations tab, and Vaccinations. (If a vaccine is missing or expired, the card also shows Add a vaccination record.)
  2. Find the vaccine. The list is set for dogs or cats (for example ARV - Anti-Rabies, Kennel Cough and the combination vaccines).
  3. Tap Camera, Gallery or PDF and choose the certificate.
  4. You will see Saved. The business will read the date off it.

Good to know: a file can be up to 10 MB. If a PDF is too big, try a smaller scan or take a photo of the certificate instead.

You do not type the dates yourself: the business enters the expiry date from your certificate. Adding a new certificate for a vaccine replaces the old one, and the business confirms the new date.

What the statuses mean

Each vaccine saysMeaning
Valid to 1 Mar 2027 · certificate not checkedRecorded and in date; the business has not looked at the certificate yet. This never stops a booking on its own.
Valid to 1 Mar 2027 · certificate acceptedRecorded, in date, and the business accepted the certificate. A vaccine that expires today still counts today.
Not valid · certificate rejectedThe business did not accept the certificate; their note under it says what to send instead.
Expired 1 Oct 2026The expiry date has passed.
CoveredAnother vaccine your pet had covers this one (for example a combination vaccine).
Missing / Not recordedNothing on record yet.

Under each vaccine you also see whether a certificate is on file (Certificate on file, with its name) or No certificate yet.

Tracked here lists the vaccines your business checks. Other vaccines are kept on the record but not checked at check-in.

The same tab has Preventive treatments (deworming and spot-on). Recorded on trust. No proof, and nothing is refused over these.

Booking with a vaccine missing or expired

You can still send a booking request. The app shows Vaccinations to sort out and says: You can still send this request. The business may not accept your pet until these are up to date.

Foster stays have no vaccination check.

Reminders

For the vaccines your business tracks, Aubloo reminds you 14 days before one runs out, and again on the day it runs out, asking you to add the new certificate to your pet's profile.
