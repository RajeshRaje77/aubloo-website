Troubleshooting

Quick fixes for the problems people meet most often. If none of these help, contact us.

  • I did not get a sign-in code

  • Customers cannot find my business nearby

I did not get a sign-in code

By SMS (pet owners)

  1. Check the number. Type your 10-digit Indian mobile number, without +91 or a leading 0. Use the number your pet business has for you, not the business's own line.
  2. Wait a minute. A text can take a little while, especially with a weak signal. Check your phone's SMS inbox, and any spam or blocked-messages folder it has. The message comes from Aubloo (Muktaasha Labs).
  3. Ask for a new code. There is no separate resend button: tap the back arrow and tap Send OTP again. Use the newest code; a code lasts 10 minutes.
  4. Get it by email instead. If you have confirmed an email on your account, tap Use an email code.
  5. Did your number change? Ask your pet business to change it, then tap My pet business changed my number. See Signing in.

By email

  1. Check the address you typed.
  2. Look in your spam, junk or promotions folder.
  3. Wait a minute, then send a new code. Use the newest one; a code lasts 10 minutes.
  4. Pet owners: an email code only works for an email you have already confirmed in My profile under Sign-in email. Otherwise, sign in with your mobile.

If the app says Too many attempts. Wait a minute and try again., wait before asking again. That code has expired. Send a new one. appears when a code has run out or does not match: check the digits, or ask for a new code.

A photo or video will not upload

  • Check your connection and try again. Uploads need the internet.
  • Size: a file can be up to 10 MB. For a large PDF certificate, try a smaller scan, or take a photo of the certificate instead.
  • Permission: if the camera or gallery does not open, allow Aubloo to use the camera and photos in your phone's settings.
  • Businesses, stay photos: each pet can have up to 5 photos and 1 video a day on a boarding or daycare stay. A video longer than 30 seconds is cut to its first 30.

I am not getting notifications

  1. Open the bell (Messages) in the app. Most messages from the last 30 days are there, even when an email or text did not arrive.
  2. Open My profile → How we reach you and check that Email, WhatsApp and SMS are switched on. A channel marked This business does not send on … yet is one your business does not use.
  3. For email, check that your email address is right in My profile, and look in your spam folder.
  4. WhatsApp and SMS messages go to your mobile number, the one you sign in with. Check that WhatsApp is set up on that number, and look in WhatsApp for messages from Aubloo.
  5. Marketing messages stop if No marketing messages is on; booking and stay updates still come.

Aubloo reaches you in the app, by email, on WhatsApp and by SMS: updates about your pet go out on every channel you keep on.

Customers cannot find my business nearby

Pet owners search by city, and by business name, area or PIN code. When they tap Show distances, the nearest businesses come first, and a business without a map pin goes to the end of the list.

If you run the business:

  1. Set your full address, including the city and PIN code: Settings → Contact details → Address.
  2. Set your map pin: at your premises, on the same screen, tap Set my business location. Customers searching nearby then find you first, see how far away you are, and can get directions.
  3. Check the services customers search for have a price set. A business shows up for a service only when that service is priced.
  4. Your business must be active on Aubloo. Customers search for the kind of pet you look after (dogs or cats).

If you are a pet owner: check the city spelling, try the business's name or your PIN code, and set Service to Any. Still missing? The business may not be on Aubloo yet: ask them to join.

"Couldn't reach Aubloo"

The app could not talk to Aubloo's servers. Nothing was changed.

  1. Check you are online: turn Wi-Fi or mobile data off and on, or try another network.
  2. If you use a VPN or a work or school Wi-Fi, try without it.
  3. Try again in a moment.

You may also see Aubloo is taking longer than usual to answer. Try again in a moment. or Aubloo had a problem at its end. Nothing was changed — try again in a moment. Both mean: wait a little and try again. If it keeps happening, contact us.

Updating the app

Update Aubloo like any other app: from the Google Play Store on Android, or the App Store on iPhone. Search for Aubloo and tap Update.

If the app shows Time to update Aubloo, your version is too old to keep running safely. Install the latest version from your store; your data is safe and waiting.

To see which version you have: My profile → About Aubloo → Version.
