Business location

Pet owners find you by your city, area and PIN code, and by distance if you have saved your location. Your address is required. Your location is optional, but it helps.

Your address

You enter it in the setup wizard. To change it later, open Settings → Contact details → Address.

  • Address line 1: flat, building, street.
  • Address line 2 (optional): a landmark.
  • Area or locality: how customers will look for you. If other businesses in your city already use a spelling for your area, Aubloo suggests it. Pick it, so customers searching that area find you all together.
  • City and State.
  • PIN code: six digits, not starting with a zero.

Tap Save. Customers search by area and PIN code, so those two are worth getting exactly right. Until your address is complete, your dashboard shows Customers cannot find you.

Your location

There is no map to drag a pin on. Aubloo saves your phone's current position, so do this while you are at your premises.

  1. Open Settings → Contact details.
  2. Under Where you are, on a map, tap Set my business location, and allow location access if your phone asks.
  3. You see Location saved. To change it later, tap Update business location.

Your exact position is never shown to customers. Customers searching nearby see how far away you are, and your own customers can get directions to you. Without a location, your dashboard reminds you with No map pin yet.

How customers find you

In the app, a pet owner opens Find your pet business. They enter a city, and optionally a business name, area or PIN code, then choose dog or cat and a service. With Show distances on, the list is nearest first; businesses without a saved location come last but are still listed. The customer then asks you to take them on.

Only businesses that are live on Aubloo are listed. A customer only finds you for a service you have switched on.

Still not showing up? See Customers can't find my business nearby.

Your phone and email for customers

The same Contact details screen holds the phone, email and emergency phone your customers see on their Contact & support screen. Leave blank anything you would rather not publish.
