Deleting your Aubloo account

Effective: 2 August 2026

Google Play asks every app that lets you create an account to publish a page like this one, reachable without signing in. This is that page. The full detail is in §8 of the Privacy Policy.

Aubloo is operated by Muktaasha Labs, a sole proprietorship based in Pune, Maharashtra, India.

In the app

This is the quickest way, and it takes effect immediately.

  • If you are a pet owner: open My profile, then Delete my account.
  • If you run a business on Aubloo: open Settings, then Delete my account, under Your Aubloo account.

You will be asked to type DELETE to confirm, because it cannot be undone.

If you would rather write to us

Email admin@aubloo.com from the address on your account, and we will do it for you. We will confirm when it is done and tell you what was kept and why.

What is deleted

  • Your sign-in. You will not be able to log in again, and your password is destroyed.
  • Your name, phone number, email address and location.
  • Any notes a boarding business kept about you.

What a boarding business keeps

  • That a stay happened, what it cost, and what was paid.
  • Your pet's care and vaccination records for those stays.

These are the business's own accounting and animal-care records — the same records it would keep on paper — and it is required to keep them. They stop being linked to you, and are anonymised at the moment you delete. Nobody can look at them and see who you were.

Photos and videos from a stay are on a separate, automatic clock and are permanently deleted about 20 days after they are taken, whether or not you delete your account.

If you are the only administrator of a business

If your business still has customers and pets on its books, deleting your account would leave it with nobody able to run it — so the app will ask you to add a second administrator first, under Settings → Staff & permissions. Your account then deletes straight away.

If the business has no customers or bookings at all, there is nothing to hand over: it is removed along with your account, and you are not asked for anything.
