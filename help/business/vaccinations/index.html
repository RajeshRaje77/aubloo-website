Vaccinations

Pick the vaccines you track. Owners upload certificates, your team records the expiry dates, and check-in tells you when a pet is not up to date.

Choose the vaccines you track

  1. Open Settings → Your business → Important vaccines.
  2. Under Which vaccines do you track?, tick each vaccine you want checked. Each tick saves straight away; there is no Save button.

Where vaccines are grouped under Any one of these is enough, one current vaccine from the group covers the pet. Ticking is your policy: you can tick none. Unticking a vaccine deletes nothing from any pet's record. Untracked vaccines stay on the pet under Other vaccines and are not checked at check-in.

Aubloo maintains the list of vaccines for dogs and for cats. If you see "Nothing to tick yet", the list for your species has not been published; you can tick vaccines as soon as they appear.

Record a vaccine from a certificate

Owners add a certificate (a photo or file) for each vaccine in their app, or your team adds it with Camera, Gallery or PDF. Your team then reads the expiry date off it.

  1. Open the customer, then the pet, then the Vaccinations tab.
  2. Check the certificate with View, then tap the calendar icon (Set … expiry) and pick the expiry date. A date can only be recorded once a certificate exists.

Pets with a certificate still waiting for a date appear on your dashboard as "certificates waiting for a date". Each vaccine shows one line: Valid to <date> · certificate not checked, Valid to <date> · certificate accepted, Not valid · certificate rejected or Expired <date>; Missing and Covered (by another vaccine in its group) are shown as before.

Your team records the dates. Aubloo does not verify certificates.

Accept or reject a certificate

The vaccine's line says whether its certificate is not checked, accepted or rejected.

  • Tap Accept when it is in order.
  • Tap Reject and say What is wrong with it?. The owner sees your note, so say what they should send instead.

A rejected certificate counts as not current. A not-yet-checked certificate never blocks anything on its own. When the owner sends a new certificate or a new date is recorded, the certificate goes back to not checked. Accepting a certificate does not make an expired date current.

At booking and at check-in

A booking request is not refused over vaccines. When you review it, each gap shows as a flag such as "Vaccination — Rabies".

At check-in (and when a groom starts), a pet that is not up to date shows a red panel … is not up to date, listing each gap: nothing on record, expired some days ago, or certificate sent but not yet checked. You can then:

  • Override and check in: the pet is checked in, and who overrode which vaccine, and by how much, is recorded.
  • Turn them away.
  • Not now.

Checking in needs the Check pets in right (see Staff). Foster stays are not vaccination-checked. If the device has no signal, the check-in is saved on the phone and you are told about any lapsed vaccine once it reconnects.
