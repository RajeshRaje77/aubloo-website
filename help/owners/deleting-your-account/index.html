Deleting your account

You can delete your Aubloo account yourself, in the app, at any time. It takes effect immediately and cannot be undone.

Delete it in the app

  1. Tap the My profile icon (the person at the top of the screen).
  2. Scroll to Your account and tap Delete my account.
  3. Read the two lists: What is removed and What a business keeps.
  4. Type DELETE in the box (capitals or not, it does not matter).
  5. Tap Delete my account. You are signed out, and you cannot sign in to this account again.

Good to know: if you are not in a business yet (on Find your pet business or Your pet businesses), tap the Settings gear instead, then Delete my account.

Or write to us

Email admin@aubloo.com from the address on your account and we will delete it for you. We will confirm when it is done and tell you what was kept and why.

What is erased

  • Your sign-in. You will not be able to log in again, and your password, if you had one, is destroyed.
  • Your name, phone number, email address, city and location.
  • Notes: any notes a pet business kept about you, and the notes you wrote on your bookings.
  • Your sign-in and verification records, your devices' notification tokens, and any change of phone number or email you asked for.
  • On your pets' records: the vet's and the emergency contact's names and numbers, the microchip number, the health and feeding notes, the special instructions and toilet habits, the photo, every uploaded document, and the vaccination certificates with their files.
  • Waiting requests: any booking request still waiting for an answer is withdrawn, and the business is told.

What a pet business keeps

  • That a stay happened, what it cost, and what was paid.
  • Each pet's name, breed, the record of its stays, and which vaccines it had and when, as the business's care history.

These are the business's own accounting and animal-care records, the same records it would keep on paper. They stop being linked to you the moment you delete: nobody can look at them and see who you were.

Aubloo also keeps the record that you accepted its terms and privacy policy (which version, and when), with nothing else about you, because it must be able to show that consent was given.

Stay photos and videos are on their own automatic clock: they are removed a few days after your pet goes home, whether or not you delete your account. Each day in your pet's stay photos shows the date it is kept until.

The full detail is in the Privacy Policy and on the Deleting your Aubloo account page.
