Adding a pet

Your pet's page holds its bookings, photos from its stays and its records. You can add a pet in a minute and fill in the rest later.

Add a pet

  1. Open your business in Aubloo.
  2. Tap Add a pet. It is in the middle of the screen if you have no pets yet, or at the end of the row of your pets' names.
  3. On the Profile tab, fill in Name *, Breed * and Sex *. Add a photo if you like: tap the photo, then Camera or Gallery.
  4. Tap Save. If something is missing, the app says what, for example Still needed: Name, Breed and Sex.

Required to save: name, breed and sex. At a cattery, also Housing with other cats (alone, not within sight of another cat, or near other cats). The photo is optional.

You do not choose dog or cat: the pet is the kind the business looks after.

Don't know the date of birth?

Under Date of birth or age, tap Know only the age?, enter the years (and months, if you know them) and tap Use this age. Aubloo saves it as an estimated date of birth, so the age stays right as your pet grows.

What the business needs before a stay

Before boarding, daycare or grooming (not foster care), the business needs:

  • Vet name and Vet phone
  • Emergency contact name and Emergency contact phone
  • Feeding times and allergies, on the Care tab

If any are missing when you book, the app lists them and offers Complete …'s profile.

The three tabs

TabWhat goes there
ProfileName, breed, sex, date of birth or age, weight, sterilised, microchip number, vet and emergency contact.
VaccinationsA certificate for each vaccine. Opens once the pet has been saved. See Vaccination records.
CareFeeding, meals a day, health conditions and medication, toilet habits (litter habits for cats), and anything else the business should know.

Change a pet's details

  1. On your pet's card, tap About … (with your pet's name).
  2. Tap Edit, change what you need, and tap Save.

If you leave without saving, the app asks Leave without saving? first.

Good to know: your business can see and update your pet's details too. A pet cannot be removed from the app today; ask your business if a pet's record needs to change.
