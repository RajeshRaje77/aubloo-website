Stay photos and video

During a boarding or daycare stay, your team can share up to 5 photos and 1 video of each pet every day. The owner sees them in their app.

Add a photo or video

  1. On your home screen, open Bookings → Calendar and find the pet's card.
  2. Tap More actions → Stay photos.
  3. Tap Take a photo or video.
  4. If you like, add a caption first. The owner sees it with the photo.
  5. Choose Camera or Gallery for photos (from the gallery you can pick several), or Record video or Video from gallery.

The page shows today's count, for example "0 of 1 video · 2 of 5 photos today". Stay photos are for boarding and daycare stays, not for grooms or foster stays.

The daily limits

  • 5 photos and 1 video per pet per day. The day is the calendar day in India time.
  • A video can be up to 30 seconds. The camera stops at 30 seconds. On Android, a longer video from the gallery is cut to its first 30 seconds. On iPhone, trim it in Photos first (Edit, then drag the ends), or record one in Aubloo.
  • A file must be under 10 MB once Aubloo has made it smaller. Aubloo shrinks photos and videos on the phone before sending them.

When a pet has today's 5 photos or today's video, the buttons are greyed out. To share a different one, delete one of today's first.

Send today's update

Once a pet has a video from today, tap Send today's update. If there are several videos, choose which one leads the update. The owner gets a "Today with …" message in their app, and the button changes to Update sent with the time.

Each new photo or video also shows up for the owner in the app; owners are told at most once an hour per pet.

How it reaches the owner today: stay photos, videos and updates are delivered in the Aubloo app. They are not sent by SMS or email.

Delete a photo or video

Tap the item, then Delete this photo (or video), and confirm with Delete. It is removed for you and the owner. Only photos and videos taken today can be deleted. A daily update already sent cannot be called back.

How long they are kept

Stay photos and videos are deleted automatically 5 days after they were taken. If a pet goes home sooner, anything from the last 5 days of the stay stays visible until 5 days after pickup. In total, a photo is kept for at most about 10 days. Owners can tap Share to save the ones they love.
