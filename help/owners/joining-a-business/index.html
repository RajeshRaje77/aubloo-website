Joining a business

On Aubloo you are always a customer of a pet business: your pets, bookings and stay photos live with that business. Here is how to link up with one, or more than one.

If your business has already added you

Many businesses add their customers themselves. When you sign in with the mobile number (or email) they have for you, Aubloo shows We found a record that may be yours, with the business's name and, often, your pets' names.

  1. Check the business and the name. Only say yes if you recognise the business and the name.
  2. Tap That's me. If it is not you, tap Not mine.
  3. The business appears under Your pet businesses. Tap it to see your bookings and pets.

Find a business and ask to join

The first time you sign in, Aubloo asks About you: your name and your city (the city where you would look for a pet business). Then:

  1. On Find your pet business, check the City.
  2. Optionally type in Business, area or PIN code (optional).
  3. Choose your Pet (Dog or Cat) and, if you like, a Service: Any, Boarding, Daycare, Grooming or Foster care.
  4. Tap Search. To see the nearest first, tap Show distances and allow your location. Your location is used only for this search and is not stored.
  5. On the business you want, tap Ask to join.
  6. Check Your name, Mobile (the number the business will have for you) and Email. You can add your pet's name and a note. Tap Send.

Good to know: money is settled with the business directly. Aubloo takes no payment.

You can have one open ask per business, and up to 10 asks a day. There is no limit on how many businesses you can join.

Cannot find your business? See The business is not in the search.

Waiting for an answer

Under the search, Your requests shows each ask:

  • Waiting for … — we will email you: the business has not answered yet. You can close the app.
  • Accepted — pull down to check again: pull the screen down and the business opens.
  • Not this time: … with the business's reason. You can ask again, or ask another business.

When the business accepts, you get a Welcome to … message. If you gave your pet's name, the pet is already on your home screen and you can book; otherwise, add your pet first.

Joining another business

  1. Open My profile.
  2. Tap Join another business and search as above.

Switching between businesses

With more than one business, tap the Switch business icon at the top of the home screen and choose one. The app changes to that business's name and colours. The back arrow (Your businesses) returns to the list.

Leaving a business: there is no button to leave a business in the app today. If you want a business to stop holding your details, ask the business, or delete your Aubloo account.
