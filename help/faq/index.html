Frequently asked questions

Short answers, with a link to the full guide where there is one.

About Aubloo

What is Aubloo?

An app for pet boarding, daycare, grooming and foster care businesses, and for the pet owners they look after. Businesses run their bookings, pets and bills in it; pet owners book, keep their pets' records, and see photos from a stay.

Does a pet owner pay Aubloo anything?

No. Aubloo takes no payment from pet owners. You pay the business directly, at the counter or as they ask, and they mark it paid in the app.

Which businesses are on Aubloo?

Each business looks after either dogs or cats. Search for yours in the app: see Joining a business.

Signing in

Do I need a password?

Not as a pet owner. Your mobile number is your account, and you sign in with a 6-digit code sent to it by SMS (or to your email). See Signing in.

Can I sign in with a number from outside India?

Not today. Pet-owner sign-in takes a 10-digit Indian mobile number.

I changed my phone number. What now?

Ask your pet business to change it. They send a code to your new number; then tap My pet business changed my number on the sign-in screen.

Does the app sign me out if I leave it?

Pet owners stay signed in until they sign out.

Pets and bookings

Can I book for two pets at once?

Yes, for a stay. A groom is booked one pet at a time.

Is my booking confirmed when I send it?

No. It is a request until the business confirms it, and nothing is held for your pet until then. See Booking.

Can I change my dates?

Not in the app. Withdraw the request and send a new one, or ring the business. An approved booking can be cancelled until the day before it starts.

My pet's vaccination has expired. Can I still book?

You can send the request, but the business may not accept your pet until the vaccinations are up to date. Add the new certificate: see Vaccination records.

Can I remove a pet?

Not in the app today. Ask your business if a pet's record needs to change.

Can I leave a business?

There is no button for it today. Ask the business, or delete your account.

Photos and messages

How long are stay photos kept?

About 10 days at most: each is kept 5 days after it was taken, and the last 5 days of a stay stay until 5 days after pickup. Then they are removed automatically. Each day shows Here until …. Use Share to save the ones you want. See Stay photos.

Do messages come on WhatsApp?

Yes, once you have signed in to the app. Booking updates, pick-up and the day's stay photo come on WhatsApp; when WhatsApp cannot reach you, booking messages come by SMS. In-app, email and SMS messages are always on; you can turn WhatsApp updates and Birthday wishes off in My profile → How we reach you. See Notifications and messages.

Your data

Who can see my pets' details?

You, the business you are a customer of, and a family member if you add their number in My profile. Read the Privacy Policy for the detail.

How do I delete my account?

In the app: My profile → Delete my account, then type DELETE. See Deleting your account for what is erased and what a business keeps.
