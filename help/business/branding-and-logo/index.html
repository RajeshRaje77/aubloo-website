Branding and logo

Your business name and logo are how you appear to your customers in the app. Open Settings → Branding.

Your business name

Edit Business name and tap Save name. It is on your bills and everything your customers see. It changes straight away; the change is recorded and Aubloo is told.

Your logo

  1. Under Logo, tap Gallery and choose your logo image.
  2. Aubloo checks it and saves it. The preview at the top shows how you appear to your customers.

Logo rules

  • A PNG, JPG or WebP image, under 8 MB.
  • At least 288 pixels on each side. 512 × 512 is best.
  • No more than twice as wide as it is tall, or twice as tall as it is wide.
  • A transparent or white background, with empty margins cropped away: margins make your logo smaller.
  • It must read on white. A logo is always shown on a white plate.

A logo is optional. Until you add one, your customers see the Aubloo mark. To take yours off, tap Remove → Remove logo. To use it again later, you upload it again.

Where it appears

Your logo appears next to your business name at the top of the app's screens, for your staff and for the pet owners who are your customers, and on the counter device's unlock screen. Emails and receipts carry your business name, not your logo.

The app's colours are the same for every business; there is no colour setting.
