1. What Aubloo is — and what it is not

Aubloo is software for running a pet boarding business. Boarding facilities use it to manage kennels, bookings, customers, pets, grooming and their own accounts. Pet owners use it to request bookings and follow a stay.

Aubloo does not board, groom, transport or care for animals. We are not a boarding facility, not a marketplace that sells stays, and not a party to the arrangement between you and a boarding business. The care of your pet, the price, the terms and the outcome are between you and that business.

Aubloo also does not process payments. Money is settled directly between the customer and the business; the app records what was agreed and what was paid.
