Aubloo — Privacy Policy

Effective: 2 August 2026 · Version: 1.1

Aubloo is an app for dog and cat boarding facilities. Boarding businesses use it to run their kennels; pet owners use it to book stays and follow their pet while it is away. This policy explains what we collect, why, who sees it, how long we keep it, and what you can ask us to do.

It is written against what the app actually stores, field by field. Where a retention period is stated, it is enforced in the database, not by a promise.

1. Who is responsible

Aubloo is operated by Muktaasha Labs, a sole proprietorship based in Pune, Maharashtra, India, which decides how the platform works. Under India's Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023 (DPDP) Muktaasha Labs is the Data Fiduciary for the data described here, and is who we means throughout this policy.

A proprietorship is not a separate legal person: "Muktaasha Labs" is a trading name over an individual, so the obligations in this policy are that individual's.

Each boarding business using Aubloo is also a Data Fiduciary for the records it creates about its own customers and their pets. It decides what to write in its own customer notes, what pricing to apply, and which photos to take. Aubloo provides the system; the business runs its own book.

Contact: admin@aubloo.com

If you are a customer of a boarding business and you want a record corrected or removed, you can contact us using the details above, and you can also ask that business directly — both routes work.

2. What we collect

2.1 Your account

  • Email address — how you sign in, and how we send you a sign-in code or a password reset.
  • Mobile number, where you give one.
  • A password, stored only as a one-way hash. Nobody at Aubloo can read it.
  • Which businesses you belong to, and in what capacity (owner, staff member, customer).

2.2 If you are a pet owner

Held by the boarding business you use, inside Aubloo:

  • Name, mobile number, an alternate number, email, and WhatsApp number where given.
  • Notes the business keeps about you, including any discount agreed and whether you have asked not to receive marketing messages.
  • Your bookings, what they cost, what you have paid, and what is outstanding.

2.3 About your pet — including health information

This is the most sensitive category in the app, and we treat it that way:

  • Name, breed, sex, date of birth, weight, whether sterilised, microchip number.
  • Medical conditions, medications, food allergies, toilet habits, feeding schedule and special instructions.
  • Vaccination records and their expiry dates, and vaccination certificates you upload.
  • Your vet's name and phone number, and an emergency contact.
  • A profile photo of your pet, if one is added.
  • Photos and short videos taken during a stay, so you can see how your pet is doing.

2.4 Where you are, when you ask us to look

  • Your device's location, only while the app is open and only when you tap to use it. The app can show you boarding and daycare near you and how far away each one is. You are asked for permission at the moment you tap; the search works fully if you say no, and it will not ask you again.
  • We do not keep it. Your position is used to sort that one list of results and is never written to your account, to an audit record or to any log. There is nothing to export or delete, because nothing is stored.
  • Distances are straight-line, not driving distances, so treat them as a rough guide.
  • A business's own address and map pin are different. A business tells us where it is so that customers can find it, and that address is shown publicly in the app. That is the business's information, not yours.

2.5 Technical and operational records

  • The device an install is bound to, so a staff member's phone stays signed in to the right facility.
  • Messages we have sent you (reminders, booking confirmations) and whether they were delivered.
  • An audit log of platform administrator actions, which exists to hold us accountable, not to profile you.

2.6 What we do NOT collect

  • No card, UPI or bank details. Money changes hands between you and the boarding business directly. Aubloo records that a payment happened and for how much — never how it was made.
  • No advertising identifiers, no third-party analytics SDKs, no tracking across other apps or websites. Aubloo does not sell data and does not share it with advertisers.
  • No background location. The app asks for your position only while it is open and only when you tap to search nearby; nothing runs in the background, and nothing follows you between sessions.

4. Who can see what

Aubloo is built so that one business cannot see another business's customers, pets or bookings. This is enforced in the database itself, on every table, not by the app being careful.

  • Your boarding business sees its own customers, pets, bookings and stay media.
  • Its staff see what their role allows.
  • You, as a pet owner, see your own pets, bookings and photos.
  • Aubloo platform administrators can reach data when operating the platform — restoring an account, investigating a fault, or acting on a legal requirement. Those actions are written to an audit log.
  • Other businesses see nothing. Your details never leave the business you booked with.

We use Supabase as our database, authentication and file-storage provider, hosted in Mumbai, India (ap-south-1), and an email provider to deliver messages. They process data on our instructions and hold it in India. We do not otherwise disclose personal data, and we do not sell it.

We will disclose data where the law compels it.

7. Your rights, and how to use them

Under DPDP you can:

  • Ask what we hold about you and get a copy.
  • Correct or complete anything wrong or missing.
  • Ask for erasure, where we are not required to keep it.
  • Withdraw consent — for marketing, at any time, as easily as you gave it.
  • Nominate someone to exercise these rights if you die or become incapacitated.
  • Complain — to us first, and to the Data Protection Board of India if we do not resolve it.

Deleting your account — in the app, in a few taps.

  • Pet owners: open My profile, then Delete my account.
  • Businesses: open Settings, then Delete my account, under Your Aubloo account.

You will be asked to type DELETE to confirm, because it cannot be undone.

What is removed: your sign-in, so you can no longer log in; your name, phone number, email and address; and any notes a business kept about you.

What a boarding business keeps: that a stay happened, what it cost and what was paid, and your pet's care and vaccination records for those stays. Those are the business's own accounting and animal-care records, and they stay because it is required to keep them. They stop being linked to you, and are anonymised at the moment you delete.

One exception. If you are the only administrator of a business that still has customers and pets on its books, deleting your account would leave that business with nobody able to run it. The app will ask you to add a second administrator first, under Settings → Staff & permissions; your account then deletes immediately. If the business has no customers or bookings at all, it is removed along with your account and nothing is asked of you.

If you would rather not use the app, write to admin@aubloo.com and we will do it for you, or see <https://aubloo.com/legal/delete-account.html>.

Write to admin@aubloo.com for any of the above.

8. Children

Aubloo is for adults running or using a boarding business. It is not directed at children, and we do not knowingly create accounts for anyone under 18.

9. Security

  • Traffic is encrypted in transit.
  • Passwords are stored only as one-way hashes.
  • Access to your data is enforced by database-level rules, evaluated on every single query, so a mistake in the app cannot widen who sees what.
  • Platform administrator accounts are separate, few, and their actions are logged.

No system is perfectly secure. If a breach affects you, we will notify you and the Data Protection Board as the law requires.

10. Changes

If we change this policy we will update the version and date at the top, and where the change matters we will tell you in the app before it takes effect. from an old version — it is asked again.

11. Complaints

Write to admin@aubloo.com. We will acknowledge your complaint and come back to you with an answer.

If you are not satisfied with our response you may complain to the Data Protection Board of India.
