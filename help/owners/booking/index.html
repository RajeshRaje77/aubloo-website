Booking

In Aubloo you send your pet business a request. The business confirms it, and nothing is held for your pet until they do.

Send a request

  1. On your pet's card, tap Book a service and choose the service. (Or tap Services to see the business's services and rates, then Request.)
  2. Which pet? Choose one or more pets; they can come together. A groom is booked one pet at a time.
  3. Which dates? Tap your drop-off day, then the pick-up day. For daycare, tap the day. Then choose a Drop-off time and a Pick-up time.
  4. For grooming, choose the groom, a preferred day and a preferred time. Struck-through times are taken.
  5. Add anything the business should know, then tap Continue.
  6. On Check and send, check the details and tap Send request.

You will see Request sent — … will confirm. We will tell you here and by email.

Good to know:

  • The calendar marks each day free, busy, full or closed. Full and closed days cannot be asked for. On a busy day the business confirms, or suggests the nearest dates.
  • Boarding is at least one night.
  • On a stay you can add a groom before your pet goes home: Add a groom before they go home?
  • A cattery offers boarding and daycare.
  • For a groom, if the business cannot do your time, they will book the nearest they can and call you.

Before you can send

For boarding, daycare and grooming, your pet's profile needs a vet, an emergency contact and feeding details. If something is missing, the app lists it; tap Complete …'s profile, fill it in, and come back. Foster care does not need them. See Adding a pet.

A missing or expired vaccination does not stop the request. The app shows Vaccinations to sort out; the business may not accept your pet until they are up to date. See Vaccination records.

The price

Check and send shows an Estimated total, line by line. The business confirms what it costs. If no estimate can be worked out, you can still send the request.

What happens next

On the cardMeaning
RequestedWaiting for the business's answer.
ApprovedThe business has confirmed it.
Not availableThe business could not take it. Their reason is shown, with Try other businesses.
Pending confirmationThe business has moved your groom's time. Tap Yes, that time works or Suggest another (they will call you).
Not answeredThe day passed before the business answered, so the request lapsed. Ask again for another day, or ring the business.
On board / In groomingYour pet is with the business now.
Cancelled, No-show, Turned awayThe booking did not go ahead. A pet turned away at the door shows the reason.

Each pet's past and upcoming bookings are under Bookings on its card.

Withdraw or cancel

  • A request not answered yet: tap Withdraw. The business will not see it. On a stay with a groom you can withdraw Just the groom or The whole request.
  • An approved booking: tap Cancel, then Cancel booking. The business is told straight away. You can cancel until the day before it starts; from the start day, ring the business.

There is no way to change the dates of a request in the app. Withdraw it and send a new one, or ring the business.

Bills and payment

Tap See the bill on a booking to see every line, Paid so far and Still to pay. Pay the business directly — at the counter, or ring them — and they will mark it paid here. Aubloo takes no payment.

If you have an email address on your account, your receipt arrives by email, with the booking number (for example B-1042) and a PDF.
