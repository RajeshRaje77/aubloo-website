Contact

For your pet, your booking or your bill, talk to your pet business. For the app itself, sign-in trouble, or your account, write to Aubloo.

Your pet business

The business looking after your pet is the one to ask about bookings, prices, payments, pick-up times and your pet's care.

  1. In Aubloo, tap the My profile icon.
  2. Under Help, tap Contact & support.
  3. You will see the business's phone, WhatsApp, email and address (with Directions), its hours, and an Emergency phone for out of hours, if the business has added them.

The same screen opens from the bell (Messages).

Write to us about the app, signing in, your account, a business subscription, or your personal data. Please write from the email address on your account if you have one, and tell us your mobile number and the business you use, so we can find you. Do not send us a sign-in code.

Aubloo does not take bookings or payments, and cannot change a booking for you. For those, contact your pet business.

Before you write, the answer may be in Troubleshooting or the FAQ.
