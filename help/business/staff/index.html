Staff

Add the people who work with you, decide what each role can do, and set up a shared counter device. Open Settings → Staff & roles. Only a Business Admin can make these changes.

Add a staff member

  1. On the People tab, tap Add staff member.
  2. Enter their Full name. Phone is optional.
  3. Pick a Role (see below).
  4. For a role that can sign in, you can add Their email, to work remotely. This is optional: Aubloo emails them a code, they create their own Aubloo account with that address, and they can then sign in from their own phone as well as at the counter.
  5. Enter their pay, then tap Add.

A person whose role can sign in needs a PIN before they can use the counter device. Until then they cannot get into the app. Payroll-only roles have no app access; their pay shows in your monthly profit and loss.

Adding another administrator

Choose Admin as the role. An administrator needs their mobile number and their email; Aubloo emails them a code to accept. They can run the business with you: everything except adding or removing admins. To hand the business over, use Make … owner under Who runs it.

Pay

  • Monthly salary: a whole number in rupees.
  • Paid per groom instead: for groomers at a grooming business. Either set Their share of each groom % and Aubloo works it out at every groom, or leave it blank and type the figure as each groom is finished.

Each person is paid one way or the other, never both. When you change someone's pay, grooms already finished keep what they were paid; the change applies from the next one.

Roles and what they can do

The Roles tab lists what each role can do. A Business Admin always has full access, and reports and settings are for Admins only.

RoleApp access
Reception, Boarding ManagerSign in at the counter with a PIN
Handler, Cleaner, GroomerPayroll only, no app access

These built-in roles cannot be renamed. To make your own, tap Add custom role, give it a Role name and tick its capabilities:

  • View the dashboard
  • Manage customers & pets
  • Create bookings
  • Approve or reject requests
  • Check pets in
  • Check pets out, and take payment for that stay
  • Capture & send stay media
  • Grooming lifecycle
  • Settle billing & record payment

Changing a role changes what everyone on it can do, and the card tells you how many people that is.

The counter device and staff PINs

A counter device is the shared tablet or phone at your front desk. It asks for a 4-digit PIN, so every check-in, payment and override carries the name of whoever unlocked it. A personal phone never asks for a PIN.

  1. Give each person a PIN: on the People tab, open Staff PINs, tap Set PIN next to their name and enter a New 4-digit PIN.
  2. On the tablet at the desk (not your own phone), signed in as the owner, open Staff PINs and tap Make this a counter device. Name it if you like, then tap Make it the counter.
  3. From the next time it opens, the device asks Who is this? and a PIN. You cannot use it as the owner any more. You can undo this from Staff & roles on another device.

Choose how the device locks: Ask every time, Ask after a few minutes (1 to 240; 15 unless you change it) or Stay unlocked. With Stay unlocked, everything done on it is recorded as whoever unlocked it, even if it was somebody else; it locks again every morning.

After too many wrong PINs, the person is locked out and the owner is told. Unlock them from Staff PINs; saving a new PIN unlocks them straight away.

Remove someone's access

On the People tab, turn off the access switch next to their name and confirm Remove access. They can no longer sign in. Their PIN is deleted, any counter device they set up is released, and they are signed out. Their record of what they did stays as it is.

Signing out when idle

For safety, a business or staff session signs out after 60 minutes with nothing tapped, typed or scrolled. Customers are not signed out this way.
