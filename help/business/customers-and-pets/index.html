Customers and pets

Customers either find you in the app and ask to join, or you add them yourself. Open Customers & pets from your home screen.

When a customer asks to join

Pet owners search for businesses in their app (see Business location) and tap Ask to join, with their name, mobile, email, a pet and a note.

  1. Open Customers & pets and tap the Join requests icon at the top.
  2. On each request, tap Accept or Turn down. If you turn someone down, give a reason; they are emailed.

Accepting makes them your customer and adds their first pet. If their mobile number already belongs to another of your customers, Aubloo asks you to ring them before accepting.

Add a customer yourself

  1. Open Customers & pets and tap Add a customer.
  2. Enter their Mobile number, Full name and Email. The mobile number identifies them everywhere. The email is how they sign in and find this record.
  3. Tap Add.

When that person signs in to Aubloo, the app asks them whether the record you made is theirs, and they confirm with That's me.

A customer's main number cannot be changed, because it is how your business identifies them. You can add Another number, for example for a family member. If a customer has lost their number and has the app, use Change their sign-in number on their page.

A customer's page

Tap a customer to see their details, anything they owe, and their pets. From the icons at the top you can edit their details, add a pet, start a New booking, or Mark do-not-rebook. A do-not-rebook flag needs a reason, which only your team sees. It does not stop a booking: whoever books them later is shown the flag and has to say why they are booking anyway.

A pet's profile

Tap Add a pet, or the edit icon next to a pet. The profile has three tabs: Profile, Vaccinations and Care.

  • Required to save: name, breed and sex.
  • Also on the profile: date of birth or age, weight, sterilised, microchip number, a photo, the vet's name and phone, an emergency contact, feeding times and allergies, meals a day, conditions and medication, toilet habits, and anything else to know.
  • Staff notes, which only your team sees: social behaviour and aggression history.

Before boarding, daycare or grooming, a pet also needs its vet, an emergency contact and its feeding details. If any are missing when you book, the app asks you to complete the profile. Foster stays do not need them.

For vaccines, see Vaccinations.

Search

Tap the Search icon at the top of the app and type a name, a pet or a mobile number. A booking reference such as B-42 also works. You only ever see your own business's results.
