Getting started

Register your business, wait for the Aubloo team to approve and switch it on, then answer a few setup questions. After that you can take bookings.

1. Create your account

  1. Install Aubloo and open it. When it asks How would you like to use Aubloo?, choose Pet business.
  2. Tap Register a business.
  3. Enter your Email and a Password of at least 10 characters, enter it again, then tap Create account.
  4. We email you a 6-digit code. Enter it and tap Confirm email.

2. Register your business

The Register your business screen has two tabs.

Business details

  • Business name and City.
  • Which pets do you look after?: Dogs or Cats. This is set once, at registration. A cat business can offer boarding and daycare only.
  • What are you subscribing to?: tick what you sell (Boarding & daycare, Grooming, Foster care). Your plan is priced by what you tick.
  • Your plan: pick the billing period and, for boarding and fostering, your slab (how many pets you can look after at once). The screen shows what you will pay. Our prices include GST.
  • GST number (optional): from your GST registration certificate. Leave it blank if you are not registered.

Personal details

  • Your name: what your staff list and reports will call you.
  • Your mobile: your own 10-digit mobile. This is how you sign in. Your business phone number comes later, during setup.

Tap Register. We send a code to your mobile by SMS to prove it is yours. Enter it and tap Verify, or tap Later to do it another time.

Joining an existing business as an administrator? Use I have a code from a business at the bottom of the form instead of registering a new one.

3. Approval

The Aubloo team reviews every registration.

  • Registered: your business is waiting for approval. There is nothing to pay yet, and you can close the app.
  • One step to go: you are approved. We email you your plan and its price, and the app shows the amount due. Contact the Aubloo team to settle the payment; payment is made outside the app. When it is received, the team switches you on. Tap Check if we've enabled you to see if you are live.
  • Live: we email you that your subscription has started. The app shows Your Aubloo plan; tap Got it.

If a registration is not approved, the app shows Registration declined and why, under What we found. This is not final: sort out what it says and get in touch, and the team will look again. The screen updates on its own if they do.

4. The setup wizard

When you are live, Get set up opens. It asks a few quick questions before you can take bookings, one step at a time. You can change every answer later in Settings. You only see the steps for the services you sell:

  1. Who works here?: add staff now or later. You are already on the list. See Staff.
  2. Boarding and daycare: how you charge (by the nights & days, or by the hour), your rates, your opening hours (When are you open?) and your capacity, which cannot be more than your plan covers.
  3. Grooming: your menu (you can start from a typical salon menu), your tables, and Which days do you groom?
  4. Foster care: your foster rate and how many pets you can foster.
  5. Which vaccines do you track?: see Vaccinations.
  6. Where do customers bring their pets?: your address (required) and, if you are at your premises, your location. See Business location.
  7. How do your customers reach you?: the phone number customers call (your own mobile is filled in; change it if customers should ring a different number) and an optional email.

On the last step, tap Done, then Go to your dashboard. Every service you sell must have a price before you can finish. The details are in Services and rates.

Signing in later

Choose Pet business. Sign in with your mobile and the code we send by SMS, or with your email and password. After a password, the app shows Verify your sign-in and asks for the code we email you. You can also tap Forgot password? on the same screen.
