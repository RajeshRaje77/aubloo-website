Notifications and messages

Your pet business and Aubloo keep you posted about requests, bookings, vaccinations and news. Here is where those messages appear, how they reach you, and the two choices you have.

Your messages in the app

Tap the bell at the top of the home screen (Messages). It shows the last 30 days, newest first. A dot on the bell means something new.

How messages reach you

Aubloo decides how each message is sent, so the ones that matter always arrive. In the app, by email and by SMS are always on. WhatsApp is your choice.

  • Bookings and pick-up (confirmed, changed, cancelled, the reminder, a closure, your pet is ready): in the app, and on WhatsApp. If WhatsApp cannot reach you, by SMS instead; if neither can, by email.
  • Records (payments and receipts, vaccinations, a closed booking, news from your business): in the app and by email.
  • The day's stay update and birthday wishes: in the app and on WhatsApp only, never by SMS.

You get one paid message (WhatsApp or SMS) for each update, never both.

Your choices: WhatsApp updates and Birthday wishes

  1. Tap the My profile icon.
  2. Under Messages, tap How we reach you.
  3. Email, SMS and In-app are shown as always on. Switch WhatsApp updates on or off. It saves straight away.
  4. While WhatsApp updates is on, Birthday wishes shows under it: switch it off if you do not want a birthday message for your pet.

WhatsApp starts when you sign in. The first time you open the app, it tells you You'll get booking updates and photos on WhatsApp. You can turn this off in My profile. Until you have signed in once, booking messages come by SMS.

With WhatsApp updates off, booking and pick-up messages come by SMS, and the day's stay update is in the app only.

If you see WhatsApp is paused by Aubloo, WhatsApp is off for everyone for now: messages come by SMS meanwhile, and your choice is kept for when it is back.

WhatsApp and SMS messages go to your mobile number, the one you sign in with.

Email needs an email address on your account: add one in My profile under How to reach you.

What you are told about

  • Your requests and bookings: approved or not, changed, cancelled, a reminder the day before, a closure that affects your booking, and a request that lapsed.
  • Pick-up: when your pet is ready to collect.
  • Payments: when a payment is recorded, with your receipt by email.
  • Vaccinations: 14 days before one runs out, and on the day it does.
  • Stays: new stay photos, in the app; and the day's update (Today with …), on WhatsApp (while WhatsApp updates is on) and in the app.
  • News from your business: closures and announcements, in the app and by email.
  • Joining: when a business accepts or turns down your ask to join (by email).

Messages about one booking are held for a few minutes before they go out, so if the business changes something twice quickly, you get the latest only.
