Help centre › For pet owners

Stay photos

During a boarding or daycare stay, the team can share photos and a short video of your pet. You see them in the app, day by day.

See your pet's photos

  1. Open your business in Aubloo.
  2. On your pet's card, look at the photo strip on the stay (it says Today and how many there are), or tap Photos.
  3. Photos are grouped by day, newest first. Tap one to open it; tap Play the video for a video.

The photo or video the team chose for the day is marked Today's update, and you get a Today with … message on WhatsApp (while WhatsApp updates is on) and in the app when the team sends it.

Keep the ones you love

Open a photo or video and tap Share. Your phone's share menu opens: save it to your photos, or send it on. The full-quality original is shared.

Photos do not stay for ever. Each one is kept for 5 days after it was taken, and anything from the last 5 days of a stay stays until 5 days after pickup: about 10 days at most. Then it is removed automatically. Each day shows Here until … with the date. Share the ones you want to keep before then.

How many?

For each pet, the team can share up to 5 photos and 1 video a day on a boarding or daycare stay. A video is up to 30 seconds.

Only the business adds photos; you can view and share them.

Nothing showing? The team may not have shared any yet. Moments the team shares during your pet's stay appear here. If you think something is missing, ask the business: see Contact.
