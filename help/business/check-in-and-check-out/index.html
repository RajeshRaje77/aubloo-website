Check-in and check-out

Check-in confirms the pet has arrived and what the owner handed over. Check-out ends the stay and settles the bill.

Check a pet in

  1. On the pet's card in the calendar, tap Check in. If the pet is here already and you are booking now, use Confirm & check in on the booking instead.
  2. The Check in … sheet opens. Go through it with the owner:
    • Vaccinations: if the pet is not up to date, the gaps are listed. See Vaccinations.
    • Confirm with the owner: the pet's feeding and health notes, read back to the owner.
    • Handed over (optional): carrier, food, bedding, toys. This is shown again at check-out, so nothing is forgotten.
  3. Tap Check in, or Override and check in if a vaccine is not current. Every override is recorded.

If the pet arrives before the booked day, the app asks whether to check them in today. The stay moves to today, keeps its length, and the customer is told.

Not taking the pet? Tap Turn them away and say why; the customer sees the reason. You can take it back later.

Checking in needs the Check pets in right. With no signal, the check-in is saved on the phone and sent when you are back online.

During the stay

  • Share photos and a daily video: Stay photos and video.
  • Record anything that happened with More actions → Incident.
  • When it is time, More actions → Notify for pickup tells the owner their pet is ready.

Check a pet out

  1. On the pet's card, tap Discharge and confirm. The confirmation reminds you of anything handed over at check-in, and warns you if this is before the booked pick-up.
  2. The … is going home sheet opens with the bill. Check it, correct anything, enter the amount received and tap Close booking. The details are in Bills and receipts.

Payment never holds up a check-out. If you tap Not now, the pet is still checked out and what is owed stays against the customer, to record later with Record a payment. If the person checking out cannot take payment, the bill stays open on Money owed.

Checking out needs the Check pets out, and take payment for that stay right.
